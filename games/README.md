# Infinite World — Games

A browser-playable 3D prototype inspired by the floating-island world generator in `Blender/scripts/create_world.py`.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser with an internet connection. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr.

## Modes included
- **Explorer:** roam the island, collect eight glowing crystals, follow four stacked stone cairns, discover the hidden grotto, and collect its turquoise crystal.
- **Survival:** gather wood with E and manage food and health.
- **Action RPG:** fight the island slimes with F or a mouse/touch click.
- **Building:** spend three pieces of wood to place a small cabin.

## World landmarks
The browser scene includes the Day 02 winding creek, waterfall, plunge pool and splash droplets; the Day 03 cedar footbridge with stringers, plank deck, bank supports and handrails; and the Day 08 cairn trail leading to the Day 09 hidden grotto entrance with a dim turquoise crystal. The grotto is a lightweight Three.js approximation of the Blender design, not a direct mesh export. Terrain and water heights are approximations of the Blender scene.

## Controls
WASD / arrow keys to move, Space to jump. On mobile, use the on-screen directional pad. In Survival, hold E near a tree to gather wood; in Action RPG, press F or click to attack; in Building, click to place a cabin.
